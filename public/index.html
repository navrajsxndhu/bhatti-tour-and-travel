Kuch Sawal Hain Dimaag Mein?

Aksar log humse ye baatein poochte hain, padh lijiye:

Q1: Trip ka rate kaise pata chalega?

Sir/Madam aap bas WhatsApp par click karke hume apna pickup point aur destination bata dijiye. Hum aapko WhatsApp message par turant sabse reasonable aur best discounted rate bata denge.

Q2: Payment kab aur kaise karni hoti hai?

Sir/Madam koi advance dene ki tension nahi hai. Jab aapka safar aaram se khatam ho jaye, aap driver ko cash de sakte ho ya Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm se UPI kar sakte ho.

Q3: Ertiga mein kitne log aur kitna samaan aa sakta hai?

Gaadi mein 6 sawariyan aaram se baith sakti hain (1 aage, 3 beech mein, aur 2 peeche) plus driver. Agar peeche 1 banda kam ho toh seat fold karke 3 se 4 bade suitcase aur bags aa jaate hain.

Q4: Raat ko ya subah jaldi gaadi mil jayegi?

Haanji bilkul! Chahe raat ke 2 baje flight pakadni ho ya subah 4 baje nikalna ho, aap ek baar WhatsApp pe time bata do, gaadi time pe pahunch jayegi.
